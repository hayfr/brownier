Inventions

One of the earliest brownies was created at the Palmer House Hotel in Chicago for the 1893 World's Fair. Bertha Palmer wanted a dessert smaller than cake and easy to pack into boxed lunches.

The original is still served today with walnuts on top and an apricot glaze.
