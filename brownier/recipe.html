One bowl, one pan

Brownie Recipe

Scale the recipe below and the ingredient amounts and recommended tray will update with it.

Recipe amount

9 servings 1 batch
Recommended tray 8 × 8-inch square pan

Each arrow adds or removes one full batch.

What you need

1 batch

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Butter1/2 cup
Granulated sugar1 cup
Eggs2
Vanilla extract1 teaspoon
Cocoa powder1/3 cup
All-purpose flour1/2 cup
Salt1/4 teaspoon

Take your time

Instructions

  1. 1

    Preheat the oven to 350°F and line the recommended pan with parchment paper.

  2. 2

    Melt the butter, then whisk it into the sugar until the mixture looks glossy.

  3. 3

    Add the eggs and vanilla. Whisk firmly for one full minute to help form a shiny top.

  4. 4

    Fold in the cocoa powder, flour, and salt just until no dry streaks remain.

  5. 5

    Pour the batter into the pan and smooth the surface with a spatula.

  6. 6

    Bake for 20 to 25 minutes. A skewer should come out with a few moist crumbs.

  7. 7

    Cool completely before cutting. This is the difficult part.
