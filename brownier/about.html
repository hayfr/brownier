Why this exists

Brownies only.
No life story.

Brownie Club is a small hobby project about one dessert done well. No newsletter, no ten-page introduction before the recipe, and no argument about whether a brownie counts as cake.

The recipe scales, the timer chimes, and the game exists purely for the twenty minutes you are waiting for the pan.
